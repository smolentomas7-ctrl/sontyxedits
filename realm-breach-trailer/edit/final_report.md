# REALM BREACH — The Final Battle · final report

1. **File:** `output/realm_breach_trailer.mp4`
2. **Resolution / aspect:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16 vertical, no letterboxing
3. **Frame rate:** 30 fps constant
4. **Duration:** 74.733 s (2242 frames); the last frame cuts cleanly into the first (loop)
5. **Encode:** H.264 High, yuv420p, CRF 16, AAC 320 kb/s 48 kHz stereo; integrated loudness {{LUFS}} LUFS, true peak {{TP}} dBTP after AAC decode (`output/verify_report.json`)

## Method
Everything on screen is generated in code; nothing is filmed, traced or taken from Roblox.

- **Characters, sets, VFX:** procedural Blender 4.0.2 scenes (`scenes/lib/*`): the warrior in three looks (early / mid / late), the Fallen God, five enemy types, the arena, dungeon depths, haunted forest, Gate of Heaven and meadow; particles, sparks, fire, lightning, god attack and dissolves are built as mesh effects per frame.
- **Animation:** analytic rigs keyed per frame (walk on uneven ground, actions, IK sword grips), cloth capes simulated with deterministic pre-roll; all timing from the song's beat grid. Impacts land 1–2 frames before their beat.
- **Rendering:** EEVEE (headless, software GL), every shot seeded from its ID. 50% previews were rendered and reviewed in motion before the final; the final renders at 0.75 scale with 8 samples and is Lanczos-upscaled to 1080 × 1920 with a light unsharp mask in the edit.
- **Edit:** `src/edit.py` (numpy/OpenCV) — cuts from `edit/timeline.json`, per-phase grade, seeded camera shake, speed ramps, the O12 freeze, white flashes only on the drop, the phase changes, the M406 god attack and the ultimate, Cinzel titles and word-group subtitles placed inside the TikTok safe zones, grain.
- **Audio:** the provided song + procedural SFX (`src/sfx.py`) placed from a beat cue sheet, Kokoro voice-over with character processing, ducking under every line, mixed to −14 LUFS (`src/mix.py`).
- **Verification:** `src/verify_output.py` (ffprobe + decode checks) and `src/preview_qa.py` (per-shot completeness, pops, flicker).

## Three weakest shots and how to improve them
1. **M208 (sparks off the pauldron, 7 frames):** the camera is 1.2 m from the impact, so the spark streaks are huge and the pauldron reads only before the hit. Improve: pull the camera back to ~2 m on a longer lens so the sparks scale down and the glancing blade stays legible.
2. **O7 (the Fallen God tilts his head, 1.5 s):** the tilt is so slow it reads as a still. Improve: a larger, eased head turn with the halo fragments drifting, plus a slow push-in.
3. **F3 (the god speaks, 5.3 s):** the longest hold in the film is almost static under the line. Improve: a slow dolly with a rack focus from the warrior's blade to the god, and cloth and halo motion to keep the frame alive.
